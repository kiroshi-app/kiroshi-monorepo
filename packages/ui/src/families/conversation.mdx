# Conversation

The transcript with an agent and everything that happens inside it — turns, the composer, the payloads a turn renders, and the interruptions that need an answer. Nothing here makes sense in a product with no agent in it; a component that does belongs to another family.

## Message

The transcript and its turns.

- **ChatEmptyState** — no conversation open yet.
- **AuthorLine** - the foot of every bubble: avatar, name, role badge, time and how an answer ended.
- **BotCreatedRow** - the line recording who created the agent a conversation opens on.
- **ConversationArrivalRow** - the line recording a companion joining a conversation.
- **ConversationEmptyState** — a conversation with no turns yet.
- **EmptyStateShell** — the frame both empty states are drawn in.
- **Mention** — an inline bot reference.
- **Message** — one authored message.
- **MessageActions** — the per-message action row.
- **MessageAttachments** — files carried by a message.
- **MessageBubble** — the message surface itself.
- **MessageQuote** — a quoted excerpt of another message.
- **MessageScroller** — the scroll container and its anchoring.
- **MessageSelection**: a message whose selected text offers Reply and Copy.
- **MessageSideContext** — the side rail attached to a message.
- **PinnedMessages** — the pinned strip above the transcript.
- **RichBlockPending** - the frame of a rich block while the agent still writes it.
- **Turn** — a full agent turn with its phases.

## Prompt

The composer.

- **PromptAttachButton** — starts an attachment.
- **PromptAttachments** — pending attachments in the composer.
- **PromptCommandMenu** — the slash-command menu.
- **PromptInput** — the composer field.
- **PromptMentionMenu** — the mention menu.
- **PromptQueue** - the tray of messages waiting above the field while an agent answers.
- **PromptReply** — the reply-to banner.
- **SelectionQuote**: a selection quoted from an answer, waiting in the input.

## Markdown

The payloads a turn renders.

- **Markdown** — the full renderer.
- **CodeBlock** — a highlighted, framed code block.
- **CodeSnippet** — the inline, chromeless variant.
- **MarkdownCode** — code inside the renderer.
- **MarkdownLink** — links inside the renderer.
- **MarkdownMath** — KaTeX blocks and inline math.
- **MarkdownMermaid** — diagrams.
- **MarkdownSpan** — the fork turning a marked span into a mention.
- **MarkdownTable** — tables and their overflow.
- **MarkdownTaskCheckbox** — task-list checkboxes.
- **IllustratedList**: numbered picks, each with a thumbnail.
- **Checklist**: a list to tick off, with progress and assignees.
- **SourcedImage**: one picture with its source and chips.
- **Callout**: a note or a warning boxed apart.
- **CollapsibleSections**: sections the reader opens one by one.
- **WebPageCard**: a page preview with Open as page.
- **Gallery**: up to five tiles, the last counting the rest.
- **OptionsCard**: options an agent lays out to explore, opening one is the pick.
- **InteractiveView**: sliders and the result they drive, sent back from the footer.
- **QuotableAnswer**: an answer whose text selection becomes a quote.

## Tools

The interruptions that need an answer.

- **ToolApproval** — approve or deny a tool call.
- **ToolQuestion** — the agent asks the reader.
- **AnsweredQuestionCard**: the answer given, posted under the question it closes.
- **ApplicationCard** — one application, its setup or connection status, bare as the install receipt.
- **ApplicationInstallTurn** — the install receipt posted in the assistant column, with the notice of a key left out.

## Routines

What a conversation runs on its own.

- **RoutinesPanel** — the activity of a conversation, its missions above its routines, on the trailing edge of its thread.
- **RoutineRow** — one routine, its trigger source, its switch and its deletion.
- **RoutineDetail** — one routine, its run history and the way to run it now.
- **RoutineForm** — the form a routine is created and edited in, inside the panel.

## Missions

A run a bot took on its own, and the conversation it came from.

- **MissionHeader** - the objective, the state, the ticket, the tools and the age of a mission, in two bands.
- **MissionEventRow** - one thing a mission recorded, a machine line or a bubble.
- **MissionMarks** - the tool glyph and the ticket line every mission surface shares.
- **MissionStatePill** - where a mission stands, said in words.
- **MissionCard** - a mission summarised as a row of the activity panel or as a card in the conversation it was opened from.

## Onboarding

The first-run screens before the first conversation.

- **OnboardingAccountScreen** - signing in to Kiroshi through the browser, or skipping it, in three states.
