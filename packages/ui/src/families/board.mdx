# Board

The objects a board draws, each at its measured size, in both themes. They take presentational props only: placing, selecting and editing them belongs to the board view.

## Components

- **BoardSticky**: a sticky in one of five colours, its author chip at the bottom, the hexagon for an agent and round initials for a person.
- **BoardShape**: a rounded rectangle or an ellipse with its label centred.
- **BoardText**: a heading, a body, or both.
- **BoardSection**: a tinted area, blue or purple, with its title chip in the top-left corner.
- **BoardImage**: a pasted image, with the source line under it when it came from a page.
- **BoardLinkCard**: a pasted link: preview, title on one line, description on two, source row.
- **BoardLibraryCard**: a Library document on the board, at rest or while dragged.
- **BoardPresence**: who is on the board: a pointer and name tag per agent and per person, the ring around the object an agent writes in or draws, and the agents drawing chip with Stop all.
