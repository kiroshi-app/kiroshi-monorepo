# Navigation

Sidebars, rails, tabs and header controls that move a reader between places. Each one owns its selected state and stays operable from the keyboard alone.

## Components

- **AppSidebar** — the space and conversation tree.
- **AppTitleBar**: the 40px window strip the conversations and settings screens share: the tab strip, then the window controls.
- **HeaderConversationButton** — opens the conversation's settings from the header.
- **HeaderIdentityButton** — opens the bot's settings from the header.
- **DocumentPanel**: a Library document opened beside the thread, its bar and the editor below.
- **LibraryDocumentView**: one Library document, read-only, under a breadcrumb back to the Library.
- **LibraryDocumentStates**: the empty document, files dropped in, a failed image import and the read-only bar of a Library document.
- **LibraryPanel**: the Library tab of a space: its header, filters, grid, empty state and loading skeleton.
- **NestedSidebarProvider** — a second sidebar provider inside an inset, deaf to the outer one's shortcut.
- **SearchResultRow** — one hit of the search palette, in the six kinds a hit can take.
- **ShareButton** — the host's title bar control that invites people into the space.
- **SettingsMenu** — the menu of a focused settings screen: back, the subject, its entries and a pinned foot.
- **SettingsRail** — the vertical section rail inside a settings dialog.
- **Sidebar** — the registry sidebar: provider, panel, rail, inset and menu slots.
- **SidebarListRow**: one roster row, companion or group, with its media, name line, preview, badge dot and strips.
- **SidebarMenuRow** — one sidebar row: icon, label, and what hangs under them.
- **SidebarResize** — the panel width, and the inner edge a reader drags to set it.
- **SpaceSwitcher** — moves between spaces.
- **TabDragThumbnail**: a tab dragged out of the title bar, drawn as a mini window, greyed where the drop is not allowed.
- **Tabs** — the registry tab bar.
- **TitleBarTabChip**: the lifted tab drawn alone, followed by the tear-off ghost and drawn by the incoming tab of the strip, greyed where the drop is not allowed.
- **TitleBarTabs**: the tab row of the title bar, a Library tab drawn from its descriptor, a kind, a name and an icon.
- **UserChip** — the account entry point.
