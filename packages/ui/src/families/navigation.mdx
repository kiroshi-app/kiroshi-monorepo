# Navigation

Sidebars, rails, tabs and header controls that move a reader between places. Each one owns its selected state and stays operable from the keyboard alone.

## Components

- **AppSidebar** — the space and conversation tree.
- **AppTitleBar**: the 40px window strip the conversations and settings screens share: the tab strip, then the window controls.
- **ExpandedSidebar**: the navigation shell's sidebar: the Space switcher, the destinations menu, Pinned and Conversations, then the space dots, the update row and the person footer.
- **HeaderConversationButton** — opens the conversation's settings from the header.
- **HeaderIdentityButton** — opens the bot's settings from the header.
- **BoardCanvas**: a Space's board in its own tab: the dot grid it pans and zooms over, the objects near the screen, the zoom control and the empty-board hint.
- **DocumentPanel**: a Library document opened beside the thread, its bar and the editor below.
- **LibraryCommentBarControls**: the two comment controls of a Library bar, the counter of open threads that opens the comments list and the Comment button with its C keycap, pressed while comment mode is on.
- **LibraryCommentComposer**: the card a new comment is written in, with the line naming what it is on, the field, the @ button and Send.
- **LibraryCommentMentionMenu**: the menu a comment field opens on @, the agents of the Space then its people.
- **LibraryCommentModeHint**: the pill at the bottom of a Library viewer while comment mode is on, worded for a document, a board or an image.
- **LibraryCommentPin**: the mark a comment thread leaves on a document, a board or an image, at rest, selected, resolved or dimmed.
- **LibraryCommentThread**: one comment thread, its first comment, its replies and its reply field, or its resolved form with Reopen.
- **LibraryCommentsList**: every comment thread of a Library item, under an Open and a Resolved tab.
- **LibraryDocumentBlockEditor**: a Library document edited block by block, each block drawn by the message bubble's own block component, with a / menu, a block handle and its menu, and drag to reorder.
- **LibraryDocumentView**: one Library document, read-only, under a breadcrumb back to the Library.
- **LibraryDocumentStates**: the empty document, files dropped in, a failed image import and the read-only bar of a Library document.
- **LibraryPanel**: the Library tab of a space: its header, filters, grid, empty state and loading skeleton.
- **MissionCapsule**: the missions of a sidebar conversation, hung under its row as a tree, each with its state mark, ticket and title.
- **MissionStateMark**: where a mission stands, as one decorative mark: a cut-out disc for the three states asking for a reader, a ring for the two that run on their own.
- **MissionsPage**: the main-area Missions page of a space: its header, the missions grouped by state and by agent, each group folding, with its empty, loading and failed states.
- **NavigationSidebar**: the expanded sidebar docked while the shell is open, and nothing in the left column while it is not, the same sidebar then floating in the peek panel.
- **NestedSidebarProvider** — a second sidebar provider inside an inset, deaf to the outer one's shortcut.
- **SearchResultRow** — one hit of the search palette, in the six kinds a hit can take.
- **ShellSidebarProvider** — the sidebar provider of every shell surface: Cmd+B or Ctrl+B toggles once per press, never on a held repeat, never inside a text field.
- **ShareButton** — the host's title bar control that invites people into the space.
- **SettingsMenu** — the menu of a focused settings screen: back, the subject, its entries and a pinned foot.
- **SettingsRail** — the vertical section rail inside a settings dialog.
- **Sidebar** — the registry sidebar: provider, panel, rail, inset and menu slots.
- **SidebarListRow**: one roster row, companion or group, with its media, name line, preview, badge dot and strips.
- **SidebarMenuRow** — one sidebar row: icon, label, and what hangs under them.
- **SidebarPeek**: the collapsed sidebar's floating panel: the expanded sidebar over the content card, full height, opened by resting on the window's left edge or on the title bar collapse icon, closed by leaving, by Escape or by docking.
- **SidebarResize** — the panel width, and the inner edge a reader drags to set it.
- **SpaceSwitcher** — moves between spaces.
- **TabDragThumbnail**: a tab dragged out of the title bar, drawn as a mini window, greyed where the drop is not allowed.
- **Tabs** — the registry tab bar.
- **TitleBarTabChip**: the lifted tab drawn alone, followed by the tear-off ghost and drawn by the incoming tab of the strip, greyed where the drop is not allowed.
- **TitleBarTabs**: the tab row of the title bar, a Library tab drawn from its descriptor, a kind, a name and an icon.
- **UserChip** — the account entry point.
