# Overlays

Surfaces drawn above the page, each owning focus while it is open. They move focus in on open, trap it, and restore it on close.

## Components

- **CompanionMenu** — everything the app can do to one companion, on the roster row, the gutter avatar and the mention pill.
- **ConfirmDialog** — the destructive-action confirmation.
- **ContextMenu** — the right-click menu.
- **ContextMenuPressTrigger** — opens that menu on a plain press, under the control.
- **DeviceApprovalDialog** — another device asking to use the reader's account.
- **Dialog** — the registry modal, portal, overlay and surface in one.
- **DialogSurface** — the dialog every settings dialog is built on, closed from the catalogue.
- **NoticeSurface** — the window's notice surface, raised against the top edge on a status mark.
- **Popover** — an anchored, non-modal surface.
- **PopoverPanel** — the popover closed down to the two anchors the app uses, wearing the popup surface.
- **SearchPalette** — the search surface, its tabs, its results and its scope.
- **Sheet** — the registry drawer pinned to one edge of the window.
- **Tooltip** — the registry hover and focus bubble, in its three parts.
- **TooltipHint** — the bubble closed down to a content, a child and a side.
