# Colors

Every swatch reads its live CSS variable, so this page follows the theme toolbar. Switch to **Theme layout → Side by side** to compare light and dark at once.

Colour tokens are declared in `tokens.json`: a light value, emitted on `:root`, and a dark override, emitted on `.dark`. Run `bun run tokens:build` after editing it. Use the Tailwind utility (`bg-primary`, `text-muted-foreground`) rather than the raw variable.

## Feedback

`--destructive-tint` and `--destructive-tint-foreground` are the selected and hovered fill of a destructive row and the label and icon on top, at 4.5:1 or more in both themes: the Danger zone entry of the settings menu and rail. `--destructive` alone stays a text colour, never a surface with text on top.

<ColorTokenGrid tokens={FEEDBACK_TOKENS} />

## Window

`--window-close` is the close caption surface under the pointer, identical in both themes, with a white glyph on top.

<ColorTokenGrid tokens={WINDOW_TOKENS} />

## Transcript

`--transcript-new-mark` draws the new-message separator rule, a translucent blue that is never a text colour. The label beside it reads in `--bot-badge-attention`, which clears the text floor on `--background` in both themes.
