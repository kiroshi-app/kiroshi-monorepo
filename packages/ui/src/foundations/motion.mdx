# Motion

## Easing

**`globals.css` declares one easing token, `--ease-out: cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`, and the `ease-out` utility resolves to it.** Anything that enters, leaves or answers a press uses it. `ease-in-out` and `ease-linear` keep the Tailwind defaults. The system never uses `ease-in`. The matching `EASE_OUT` curve for Motion lives in `lib/ease.ts`.

## Enter animations

Animations come from `tw-animate-css`, imported in `globals.css`. Utilities are composable: `animate-in` plus one or more of `zoom-in`, `spin-in`, `slide-in-from-*`, with `duration-*` and `ease-*` to tune them. The `animate-out` counterparts exist for exit transitions.

**Fades are for modals only.** A dialog and its backdrop fade and scale in — that is what says the page behind went away. `settings-styles.ts` owns it for the confirm dialogs, and the overrides in `globals.css` tune the vendored `ui/dialog.tsx` for the others. Everywhere else the system does not fade in: popovers, menus, tooltips, rows and bubbles appear outright, and what moves does so by transform or by clip. Outside a modal, reach for a transform, never for opacity. Five exceptions are drawn. A transcript row that arrives live fades and lifts 8px into place (`animate-thread-arrive`). The jump-to-latest button fades as it slides. A menu, a popover and the settings select leave by shrinking to 0.98 through a 4px blur and a fade, the recipe a dialog leaves by, while a tooltip leaves outright. A glyph that swaps in place, copy to check, crosses through scale, blur and opacity (`icon-swap.tsx`). The check of a chosen question option fades in place.

## Reduced motion

**`globals.css` shortens every `animate-in` and `animate-out` utility, every button and the switch thumb to `0.01ms` under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.** Everything else is the component's job: pair every other `animate-*` or `transition-*` utility with its `motion-reduce:` counterpart, as the previews on this page do.

```tsx
<div className="animate-in zoom-in-95 motion-reduce:animate-none" />
```
